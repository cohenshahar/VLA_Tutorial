## What We Did

### 1. Root Cause: Joint A1 stuck at exactly 3°

After multiple sessions of diagnostics, the arm would not move past ~3° no matter
how high the actuator torque was set. The full debugging trail:

| Test | Finding |
|---|---|
| Spring-lock A2-A6, gravity=0 | A1 stuck at 3°, A2 drifted 41° (spring-lock fails under gravity) |
| Tight joint-limit lock, gravity=ON | A2 stays put; A1 **still** stuck at 3° |
| Kinematic force-lock (qpos/qvel reset every step) | A2 locked perfectly; A1 **still** stuck at 3° |
| Printed `jnt_range` at runtime | `[-0.0518, +0.0518]` rad — **only ±3°!** |

**Root cause:** `arm.xml` `<compiler>` was missing `angle="radian"`.  
MuJoCo's default is `angle="degree"`, so range values like `2.96706` (which are in
radians = ±170°) were re-converted as if they were degrees → ±0.0518 rad = ±3°.
The arm was hitting a hard joint limit on every run.

**Fix — one attribute:**
```xml
<!-- Before -->
<compiler meshdir="..."/>
<!-- After -->
<compiler meshdir="..." angle="radian"/>
```

After the fix:
```
t=0.50s  θ=+23.18°  ω=+60.9°/s
t=1.00s  θ=+53.20°  ω=+60.0°/s
Settled at t=1.24s  θ=60.45°  (target 60.0°)
Overshoot: +0.45°  (0.7%)   Settling: 1.12s
```

### 2. Controller design (tune_j1.py)

Tuned a position PD controller for joint A1, with a 1-second ramp to avoid
torque spikes and a physics-settle exit condition:

| Parameter | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| KP | 800 N·m/rad | position gain |
| KV | 80 N·m·s/rad | velocity gain |
| DAMPING | 10 N·m·s/rad | joint viscous damping |
| ARMATURE | 0.5 kg·m² | motor rotor inertia |
| RAMP_DURATION | 1.0 s | setpoint ramps 0→target |
| MAX_DURATION | 10.0 s | safety cap |

**Critical lesson:** When overriding `gainprm[0]` at runtime, `biasprm[1]` must
also be set to `-KP`. Otherwise the equilibrium point shifts by factor `KP_new/KP_xml`.

**Locking other joints:** Spring-locking fails when gravity torque exceeds the
spring force at setpoint (e.g. A2 at -45° has 174 Nm gravity). Kinematic locking
(force `qpos` and `qvel` to target before each `mj_step`) is reliable.

## Key Lessons Learned

1. **MuJoCo `angle` default is `degree`** — if your MJCF stores angles in radians,
   always add `angle="radian"` to `<compiler>` or every range/axis value is wrong.

2. **`biasprm[1]` must equal `-gainprm[0]`** for position actuators — they are set
   independently at runtime. Forgetting this shifts the equilibrium angle.

3. **Spring-locking is unreliable under gravity** — use kinematic locking for
   joint-isolation tests.

4. **`jnt_range` in radians is the fastest sanity check** — print it at startup if
   the arm won't move.
